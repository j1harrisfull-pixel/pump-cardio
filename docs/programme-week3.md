**EMOM rule.**
- Minute 1 is the complex on the left, minute 2 the same complex on the right, minute 3 two-hand swings.
- The rotation is the same every round, and sides alternate by minute, with no mid-minute switch.
- Each minute gives about 35–40 s of work and at least 15 s of rest.

**Coherence.**
- Push: bench and half-kneeling press on Mon, KB press on Sat.
- Pull: chin-up and row on Mon.
- Squat: split squat and goblet squat on Thu, front-rack squat on Sat.
- Hinge: RDL on Thu, swings on Sat.
- Carry: farmer's carry on Mon only.
- Core: side plank on Thu.
- Two strength days plus bell work, about 75 min of Zone 2, and two genuine recovery days around the hospital test.
- Every session differs from its week-2 counterpart (checked by `tests/test_v30.py`).
