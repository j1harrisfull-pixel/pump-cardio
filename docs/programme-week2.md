# Programme – Week 2 (Mon 19 – Sun 25 Oct 2026)

Added straight into the session data in `src/app.js` (pc-v29) by the Health & Fitness bot. Week 1 (12–18 Oct) is unchanged.

**Standing rules (unchanged).** Strength work is at RPE 6–7 with 2 reps in reserve (2 RIR). No breath-holding or Valsalva, no head-down or inverted positions, no heavy spinal loading (no barbell back squat or conventional deadlift), no maximal or heavy barbell lifts, and no snatches to fatigue. Effort words are single words with their bands: easy RPE 3–4, steady 5–6, moderate 7. Every session shows the stop-signs line. Endocrine admission is Wed 28 Oct, so this week builds gently and finishes fresh.

**Progression basis.** No weights or reps were logged for week 1 (`wo_wt` empty; no logs in the bot inbox). So each step is small and stated relative to the week-1 prescription:
- one more rep, or
- a slower tempo with a pause, or
- a little less assistance, or
- about 2 kg on the anchor lift.

"Last: kg" on each card fills in automatically once James logs.

**Session shape (Functional Bodybuilding style).**
1. Warm-up flow.
2. **One compound anchor** in straight sets (rest 60–120 s).
3. An accessory superset.
4. A stability/mobility pairing (carry + control move).
5. A short conditioning piece or flush.
6. Active stretches.

Strength sessions run about 35 min.

## Rationale per day

**Mon 19 – rest, optional hips + ankles flow.**
- The day after the week-1 long walk is kept for recovery.
- The flow is lower-body focused (ankles, hip flexors, glutes, 90/90). It has no shoulder work, so it doesn't overlap Thursday.
- It's built from different moves than week 1's Thursday flow, which was CARs-led.

**Tue 20 – Upper. Anchor: flat dumbbell bench press.**
- *Why this compound:* week 1 used an incline press at 3011. A flat dumbbell bench is the natural next horizontal press. It loads more of the chest, keeps a stable back-supported position with no spinal load, and lets each side work independently.
- *Progression:* about 2 kg above last week's incline weight, as straight sets of 6 with a 1 s pause (3110). The pause gives more time under tension without more load.
- *Accessories:*
  - Assisted chin-ups move from C to B, with a little less leg help.
  - The chest-supported row keeps the same weight but goes from 8 to 10 reps.
  - Pulling volume (6 sets) is now higher than pressing (4 sets), which is good for shoulder health.
- *Stability pairing:* the farmer's carry (the week's only bilateral carry) plus wall slides for overhead control. Wall slides replace week 1's lateral raises and curls, so there's less isolation work.
- *Finisher:* shorter, crisper row intervals (5 × 0:30 / 0:30, moderate RPE 7) instead of 3 × 0:50.

**Wed 21 – Zone 2 Run, 5–6 km.**
- James ran an easy 5 km outside on Wed 14 Oct (about 31 min, logged as "run instead"). So the run is set at a 30:00 steady Zone 2 effort, with "aim for 5–6 km" in small print and walk breaks allowed. No pace is given.
- *Progression:* 30:00 continuous Zone 2, against 15:00 on week-1 Monday plus his own 5 km.
- *Time note:* with the warm-up and cool-down walks, the session is about 40 min, slightly over the 35-min target. The 5–6 km target needs about 30 min of running, and the walks are short and easy.
- Backup is a Zone 2 row plus floor core. Run instead turns that row into a 20:00 Zone 2 run.

**Thu 22 – rest, optional shoulders + spine flow.**
- This sits between the upper day and the lower day and focuses on what Tuesday loaded: wrists, shoulders, chest, lats and thoracic spine.
- The side-lying windmill is new, with a How-to. All positions are standing, kneeling or lying on the side or back, so nothing is head-down.

**Fri 23 – Lower + core. Anchor: rear-foot-elevated split squat.**
- *Why this compound:* it gives single-leg strength without spinal load. It progresses week 1's offset reverse lunge, and it's held at the rack or with light dumbbells.
- *Progression:* 3 × 6 each side at 3110, a longer stance range and a pause.
- *Hinge:* the dumbbell RDL moves from a C finisher to the main B superset. It uses last week's weight with a slower 3111 tempo, so there's more stimulus without more load. Cues keep the head above the hips.
- *Core:* bird-dog (anti-rotation) replaces week 1's dead bug.
- *Stability pairing:* the suitcase carry (the week's only one-sided carry) plus single-leg glute bridges.
- *Recovery for Saturday:* hinge volume is kept moderate (3 sets), with no swings and an easy 4:00 row flush. That leaves the hips fresh for Saturday's bell work.

**Sat 24 – guided single-bell EMOM + short run.**
- This progresses Saturday 17 Oct's 15-min KB flow (Every 2:00, 45 s per side). It's now a clean → front-rack squat → press complex, one side per minute, alternating with two-hand swings.
- Every window leaves at least 15 s of rest (35 s of work in each 60 s window). The timer cues left, switch → right side, and swings.
- One 16 kg bell only. No carries, so carries are never doubled.
- It's followed by a 15:00 Zone 2 run (steady RPE 5–6) and calf, hip and thoracic stretches.
- The backup needs no bell: a Zone 2 row plus a bodyweight circuit. Run instead swaps the row for a run of the same time.

**Sun 25 – longer easy walk + stretch.**
- The walk goes from 25:00 to 30:00 at easy RPE 3–4, then a new 5-move stretch.
- Run instead is 30:00 of Zone 2 running with walk breaks.
- The backup is a 15-min gentle stretch, distinct from week 1's long-hold routine.

## How the week fits together
- **Pattern balance:**
  - Push: Tue bench, Sat press.
  - Pull: Tue chin-up and row.
  - Squat: Fri split squat, Sat front-rack squat.
  - Hinge: Fri RDL, Sat swings.
  - Carry: Tue farmer's, Fri suitcase.
  - Core and stability: bird-dog, single-leg bridge, wall slides.
- **Hard–easy rhythm:** strength days (Tue, Fri) are separated by an aerobic day and a recovery day. Saturday's bell work comes after Friday's moderate hinge volume, and Sunday stays easy.
- **Aerobic base:** about 75 min of Zone 2 running across Wed and Sat, plus the Sunday walk. Intensity is capped at moderate RPE 7 in short row intervals only (Tue).
- **Pre-admission:** nothing new is maximal, and everything is breathe-out-on-effort. The last strength day is Fri 23, five days before the Wed 28 admission.
